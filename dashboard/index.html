      How to read this dashboard

      The 50 metrics sit in eight layers. The first five layers describe the market (trend, dips, volatility, breadth, rotation); the last three are lead indicators (sentiment and calendar, credit and rates, macro). Three composites sit on top: the regime light sets the maximum TQQQ allocation, the dip-buy score grades how washed out the market is, and the heat score grades how stretched it is.

      Dip-buy components: drawdown from the 52-week high, RSI(14), RSI(2), Bollinger %B, VIX level and percentile, VIX/VIX3M, share of S&P 500 members above the 200-day, put/call. A fast widening of high-yield spreads subtracts up to 15 points. Heat components: distance above the 200-day, RSI(14), %B, low VIX, low put/call, frothy breadth, advance-decline divergence, QQQ/IVV stretch and sessions since the last 5% pullback.

      Volatility drag: a daily-rebalanced 3× fund lags three times the underlying's compounded return by roughly 3σ² a year, so 20% realized vol costs about 12% a year and 40% costs about 48%.

      Everything here is computed from public data and rule thresholds in the configuration file; it is information, not investment advice, and the thresholds are starting points to tune.
